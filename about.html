About All Day Breakfast Food

All Day Breakfast Food is a Charleston guide with one narrow job: keeping track of the kitchens that will still make you breakfast after the usual cutoff. Visitors sleep in, shift workers eat on a different clock, and plenty of locals simply prefer eggs at two. The guide exists for all of them.

Listings cover the peninsula and the towns around it, organized by area so you can find something close to where you already are. Each entry is a restaurant that serves its morning menu for as long as it is open, not one that offers a weekend brunch window.

One Recommendation for Downtown

Downtown gets a single pick on purpose. Parking is tight and most people there are walking, so a long list is less useful than one dependable answer. Ours is Sweet Palm Coffee & Food at 471 King Street, a counter-service cafe where breakfast sandwiches, burritos and egg bites are made all day and the coffee program is strong enough to be the reason for the visit by itself.

How the Rest of the List Comes Together

Outside the peninsula the guide casts a wider net, from Early Bird Diner in West Ashley to Lost Dog Cafe on Folly Beach and The Junction Kitchen & Provisions in Park Circle. A place is listed when its own menu or published hours show morning food available throughout the day. National chains appear in their own short section because at midnight they are often the only griddle running.

The popular restaurants page steps outside the breakfast brief to cover Charleston's well-known dining rooms with large menus, for the meals that come after. The dish guide works the other way, starting with the plate and pointing to who makes it.

Hours and Menus Change

Restaurants adjust their schedules by season, and a kitchen that served pancakes until three last spring may close at two this winter. Treat the times here as a starting point and confirm with the restaurant before making a special trip.
